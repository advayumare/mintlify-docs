The Tasks module helps you organize and track all your to-dos within the CRM. Tasks can be associated with **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Leads**, or **Deals**, so every follow-up and action item is connected to the right record and nothing falls through the cracks.

### Task views

The Tasks module provides three views to help you focus on what's relevant:

- **All Tasks** — See every task across the system.
- **By Status** — Filter tasks by their current progress stage.
- **Assigned to Me** — View only the tasks assigned to you.

## Create and manage tasks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a task">
    Click the **+ Create** button. Enter the task title, description, priority, and due date. Assign the task to a team member and set an initial status (for example, **Todo**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a record">
    Associate the task with a **Contact**, **Company**, **Lead**, or **Deal** so it appears in that record's activity timeline and your team has full context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    Update the task status as work moves forward — from **Todo** to **In Progress** to **Done**. Use filters in the task list to monitor your team's workload by status or assignee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the task">
    Mark the task as **Done** once the action item is finished. Completed tasks remain logged in the system for future reference and audit purposes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example use cases

- **Sales follow-up** — Create a task to call a lead after a product demo.
- **Recruitment** — Schedule reminders for interview follow-ups with candidates.
- **Customer success** — Assign tasks to team members to resolve open client issues.

## Best practices

- Always set a **due date** when creating a task so deadlines are visible to everyone involved.
- Assign tasks to specific team members to establish clear accountability.
- Use **priority levels** to help your team identify what to work on first.
- Check the **Assigned to Me** view regularly to stay on top of your own workload.
