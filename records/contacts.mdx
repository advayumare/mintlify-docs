The Contacts module lets you manage people and individual relationships within your CRM. Contacts are associated with **Leads**, **Companies**, and **Deals**, giving you a complete view of every interaction and helping you build stronger relationships with prospects and customers.

## Add a new contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Thread CRM account">
    Open Thread CRM and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Contacts">
    Select **Contacts** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click the **Create** button to open the new contact form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter contact details">
    Fill in the required fields: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a company (optional)">
    Associate the contact with an existing **Company** record to keep your data organized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the contact">
    Click **Create**. You are redirected to the contact details page where you can add more information and view activity history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Link every contact to their respective company so your data stays clean and navigable.
- Use tags such as "Decision Maker", "Influencer", or "Prospect" to identify each contact's role in the buying process.
- Regularly review and update your contact database to keep information accurate.
- Add contacts to **Email Sequences** to nurture relationships over time with automated, personalized outreach.
