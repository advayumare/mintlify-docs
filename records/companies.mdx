The Companies module lets you manage all the organizations you interact with. Companies act as the parent entity to which **Contacts**, **Leads**, and **Deals** are associated, giving you a complete account-level view of every relationship and opportunity.

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Centralized company database" icon="database">
    Store all your organizations in one place for a unified view of your accounts.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Multi-source capture" icon="upload">
    Add companies manually, import via CSV or Excel, or sync from integrations.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Company enrichment" icon="sparkles">
    Automatically populate details such as website, industry, employee size, and revenue.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Contact association" icon="users">
    Link multiple contacts to a single company for complete visibility into each account.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Activity timeline" icon="clock">
    Track all interactions linked to a company, including emails, calls, meetings, and notes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Create a company manually or bulk import from a CSV or Excel file. Companies can also be created automatically when new contacts are imported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrich and organize">
    Add company details such as name, domain, industry, size, and address. Associate contacts and leads with the company to build a complete account profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage">
    Log meetings, notes, and calls against the company. Review the company-level activity history to stay informed before every interaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate with deals">
    Link companies directly to deals to track revenue opportunities at the account level.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Thread CRM account">
    Open Thread CRM and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Companies">
    Select **Companies** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click the **Create** button to open the new company form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter company details">
    Fill in the fields: **Company Name**, **Website**, **Annual Revenue**, **Industry**, **Account Owner**, and **Country**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the company">
    Click **Create**. You are redirected to the company details page where you can associate contacts, leads, and deals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Always associate contacts with their company so your account data stays clean and easy to navigate.
- Use company domains for email association — for example, all emails from `@company.com` can be automatically linked to the correct company.
- Keep company details up to date for accurate reporting and segmentation.
