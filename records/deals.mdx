The Deals module helps you track opportunities, move them through your sales pipeline, and close more business. Deals are linked with **People** and **Companies** so you always have the full context behind every opportunity.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a deal">
    Add a deal manually or convert one directly from a qualified **Lead**. Set the deal name, value, stage, and owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate with entities">
    Link the deal to a **Contact** and **Company**. Attach related tasks, notes, and emails so all relevant information is in one place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    Move the deal through pipeline stages as it advances. Monitor the deal value and expected close date to keep forecasts accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the deal">
    Mark the deal as **Closed Won** or **Closed Lost**. For lost deals, record the reason so you can analyze patterns and improve future performance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a deal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Thread CRM account">
    Open Thread CRM and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Deals">
    Select **Deals** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click the **Create** button to open the new deal form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter deal details">
    Fill in the **Deal Name**, **Value**, and **Deal Stage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate a company and contact">
    Link the deal to a **Company** and select a **Point of Contact** from the list of contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an owner (optional)">
    Select a team member from the **Deal Owner** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the deal">
    Click **Create**. You are redirected to the deal details page where you can track activity and update the deal as it progresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Convert qualified **Leads** into **Deals** promptly to keep your pipeline accurate and up to date.
- Always set an **Expected Close Date** so your revenue forecasts reflect real timelines.
- Update deal stages regularly to reflect actual progress rather than letting deals sit in the wrong stage.
- Review won and lost deals periodically to identify patterns and refine your sales strategy.
