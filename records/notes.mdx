The Notes module lets you quickly capture and organize important details across your CRM. Notes can be linked to **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Leads**, and **Deals** so your team always has the context they need, right where they need it.

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rich note creation" icon="file-text">
    Add notes with text, links, and references to capture everything relevant from a conversation or meeting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Entity association" icon="link">
    Link notes directly to CRM records — Contacts, Leads, Deals, or Companies — so context is always attached to the right record.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Edit and delete" icon="pencil">
    Update notes when details change or remove ones that are no longer relevant.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search and filters" icon="search">
    Search notes by title or filter by created date and associated record to find what you need quickly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Activity tracking" icon="clock">
    Each note displays the author and timestamp so you always know who wrote what and when.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create and manage notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a note">
    Click the **+ Create** button. Enter a descriptive title and write the note body. Include any relevant links or references.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate with a record">
    Link the note to a **Lead**, **Deal**, **Contact**, or **Company** so it appears in that record's activity timeline and is accessible to your whole team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage existing notes">
    Use the **edit icon** to update a note when details change. Use the **delete icon** to remove notes that are no longer needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review notes">
    Browse notes in the card layout for a quick overview. Check the author and timestamp on each note to understand when information was recorded and by whom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example use cases

- **Sales follow-up** — Log call notes after speaking with a lead so the next rep who picks up the conversation has full context.
- **Recruitment** — Keep interview impressions and candidate notes in one place linked to the relevant contact record.
- **Customer success** — Document meeting outcomes and action items tied to a company or deal for your whole team to see.
- **Deal progress** — Record negotiation updates and changes in scope directly on the deal record.

## Best practices

- Always link notes to the correct record so your team can find them without searching.
- Write descriptive titles such as "Follow-up call with Jane – Pricing Discussion" so notes are easy to identify at a glance.
- Include links and relevant email snippets in notes to make them actionable.
- Keep notes current — update them after every meaningful interaction so your team stays aligned.
