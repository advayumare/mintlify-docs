The Leads module helps you manage your sales pipeline by centralizing all your leads from different sources into one place. It is designed to simplify lead capture, tracking, assignment, and conversion.

A lead represents a potential customer who has shown some level of interest in your products or services. Leads can come from a variety of channels, including:

- Website inquiries (contact forms, live chat)
- Referrals from existing customers
- Networking events and conferences
- Paid marketing campaigns (social media ads, search engine ads)

## 1. Capturing lead data

Leads can be captured from multiple sources to ensure you never miss an opportunity.

### Manual entry

Add a new lead directly from the Create Lead screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Thread CRM account">
    Open Thread CRM and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Leads">
    Select **Leads** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click the **Create** button to open the new lead form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter lead details">
    Fill in the basic details: **Name**, **Email**, **Company**, and **Phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a source">
    Choose the lead's origin from the **Source** dropdown (for example, Web Form, LinkedIn, or Import).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an owner (optional)">
    Select a team member from the **Lead Owner** field to assign responsibility for this lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the lead">
    Click **Create**. You are redirected to the lead details page where you can add notes, tasks, and activity history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Bulk import

Upload CSV or Excel files to import large volumes of leads at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import screen">
    Navigate to **Leads**, click the **More Options** menu, and select **Import Leads**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Browse for your CSV or XLSX file and click **Upload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map fields">
    Match the columns from your uploaded file to the corresponding fields in the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the import">
    Check the preview of your data to confirm everything looks correct before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the import">
    Click **Import**. Your leads are imported and available in the Leads list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Deduplication

The system automatically checks for existing records before adding new ones, preventing duplicate leads from cluttering your database.

## 2. Managing leads

Once captured, leads can be enriched, organized, and tracked throughout their lifecycle.

**Activity timeline** — Track all interactions associated with a lead, including calls, emails, notes, and meetings, in chronological order.

**Segmentation and filters** — Group and filter leads by:

- Source
- Stage
- Priority
- Tags

Use filters to quickly surface the leads that need your attention most.

## 3. Converting leads to deals

Qualified leads can be converted into deals with a single click.

**Conversion options** — When converting a lead, you can:

- Convert the lead into a **Deal**.
- Link the deal with existing **Contacts** or **Companies**.

**Audit trail** — A full history of lead-to-deal conversions is preserved so you can track how each opportunity originated.

**Pipeline integration** — Converted deals automatically appear in your **Sales Pipeline**, where they can be moved through stages and tracked toward close.

## 4. Adding leads to email sequences

Leads can be added to automated **Email Sequences** for consistent, scalable engagement.

- **Add to sequence** — From the lead profile, select **Add to Sequence** and choose a predefined campaign.
- **Personalization** — Use dynamic fields to personalize emails with the lead's name, company, and other details.
- **Tracking and reporting** — Monitor open rates, replies, and click-through rates directly from the lead's activity timeline.
- **Automation** — Leads progress through the sequence automatically until they respond, convert, or are removed.

## Best practices

- Regularly clean and deduplicate your leads list to maintain data quality.
- Use smart filters to prioritize high-value leads and focus your team's effort.
- Align email sequences with lead stages so your messaging matches where each lead is in the buying journey.
- Convert leads to deals promptly once they are qualified to avoid missed opportunities.
