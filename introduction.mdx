Thread CRM is a platform built for teams that sell to other businesses. It gives you a single place to track every contact, account, deal, and conversation — from the first lead to a closed customer.

B2B sales are complex: cycles are long, multiple stakeholders are involved, and data gets scattered across spreadsheets and inboxes. Thread CRM centralizes all of that so your team can stay aligned and move faster.

## Key features

**Contact & account management** Store and organize all your business contacts and company accounts in one place. Track interaction histories, organizational hierarchies, and key details without hunting through emails or spreadsheets.

**Lead & opportunity management** Move leads through your pipeline from initial contact to qualified opportunity. Visualize your entire sales funnel and manage stages with a drag-and-drop interface.

**Sales automation** Automate repetitive work like lead assignment, follow-up reminders, and data entry. Your team spends less time on admin and more time building relationships.

**Marketing automation** Create and manage outreach campaigns, segment your audience, send targeted emails, and track the effectiveness of each campaign.

**Integrations** Connect Thread CRM to the tools you already use — email platforms like Gmail and Outlook, collaboration tools like Slack and Microsoft Teams, accounting software like QuickBooks and NetSuite, and more. The REST API lets you build custom integrations for workflows specific to your business.
