Follow these steps to get your Thread CRM workspace up and running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up or log in">
    Go to [app.threadcrm.ai](https://crm.b2bleadsmarket.com) and create your account or log in to an existing one. If your organization already has a workspace, ask your admin to invite you instead of creating a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your workspace">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → General** to set your workspace name and basic preferences. This is also where you configure your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and other team-wide settings.

    <Tip>
      Set up your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) in workspace settings early. Defining your target customer criteria helps your team qualify leads consistently from the start.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → Members** to invite your colleagues by email. Assign each member a role that matches their responsibilities.

    See [Members & Roles](/settings/members-roles) for a full breakdown of available permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import or create your first records">
    Add your existing data by importing a CSV or Excel file on the [Leads](/records/leads), [Contacts](/records/contacts), or Companies pages. You can also create records manually from any of those pages.

    <Note>
      Each record type serves a different purpose. Leads are early-stage prospects, contacts are individuals you have a relationship with, and companies group contacts under a single account.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your email account">
    Go to **Settings → User Settings → Email Accounts** to link your Gmail or Outlook account. Once connected, emails you send and receive are automatically logged against the relevant records.

    See [Email setup](/settings/email-setup) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate an API key (optional)">
    If you plan to integrate Thread CRM with other tools or build custom workflows, go to **Settings → Developer → API Keys** and generate a key.

    See the [API Keys](/settings/api-keys) settings page or the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) for details on authenticating requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>
