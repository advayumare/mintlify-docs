## Get started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Sign up at [app.threadcrm.ai](https://app.threadcrm.ai) and configure your workspace settings, including your team name and branding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → Members** to invite teammates via email or a shareable link, and assign them roles with the appropriate permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your data">
    Use the **Import** feature on People, Leads or Companies to upload your existing CSV or Excel data and start managing your pipeline right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
