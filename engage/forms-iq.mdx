FormsIQ is the built-in web form builder for Thread CRM. Use it to create forms that capture visitor information from your website and send it straight into your CRM as new lead records — no manual import, no copy-paste.

When someone submits a FormsIQ form, a lead record is created automatically with the submitted data mapped to the correct fields. Your team can see new submissions immediately and act on them right away.

## Create a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open FormsIQ">
    In the left sidebar, navigate to **Engage → FormsIQ**. Click **Create Form**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your form">
    Give the form an internal name (for example, "Contact Page Form" or "Demo Request — Homepage"). This name is visible only to your team in the CRM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fields">
    Click **Add Field** to add form inputs. For each field:

    - Choose the field type (text, email, phone, dropdown, textarea, etc.).
    - Enter a label (what visitors see).
    - Mark the field as **Required** if submission should be blocked without it.
    - Map it to a CRM field so submitted data lands in the right place on the lead record.

    Common fields to include: First name, Last name, Work email, Company, Phone, and a message or inquiry field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure form settings">
    Set a **confirmation message** or **redirect URL** to show after a successful submission. You can also set a default lead owner to assign new submissions to a specific team member automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save**, then toggle the form to **Active**. Only active forms accept submissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Embed a form on your website

After publishing, FormsIQ gives you two ways to add the form to your website:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Embed code" icon="code">
    Copy the HTML snippet and paste it into your website's source code wherever you want the form to appear.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Direct link" icon="link">
    Use the hosted form URL to link directly to the form — useful for email campaigns or landing pages where embedding isn't an option.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To get the code or link:

1. Open the form from the **FormsIQ** list.
2. Click **Embed / Share**.
3. Copy the snippet or URL.

<Note>
  The embed code uses an iframe. If your website has a Content Security Policy that blocks iframes, you may need to allowlist the CRM domain. Contact your web developer for help with CSP configuration.
</Note>

## How form data maps to lead fields

When a visitor submits the form, the CRM creates a new lead record and populates it using the field mapping you configured. Standard field mappings include:

| Form field | Lead record field |
| --- | --- |
| First name | First name |
| Last name | Last name |
| Work email | Email |
| Company | Company |
| Phone | Phone |
| Message / inquiry | Notes |

You can map any form field to any standard or [custom field](/settings/custom-fields) on the lead record. Fields that have no mapping are saved as raw submission data but do not populate the lead record automatically.

### Duplicate handling

If a submission comes in with an email address that already exists in your CRM, the system detects the duplicate and does not create a second lead record. Instead, it updates the existing record with the new submission data and logs the form submission as an activity on the timeline.

## Manage form submissions

Open any form and click the **Submissions** tab to see a log of every submission:

- Submission date and time
- The data submitted in each field
- Whether a lead record was created or an existing record was updated
- The lead record linked to the submission

You can click through to the lead record directly from the submission log.

<Tip>
  Set up an [email sequence](/engage/email-sequences) to enroll new leads automatically after a form submission. This way, every inquiry gets a prompt, consistent follow-up without any manual work.
</Tip>

## Edit or deactivate a form

To change a form's fields or settings:

1. Open the form from the **FormsIQ** list.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Make your changes and click **Save**.

<Warning>
  Removing a required field or changing a field mapping on an active form affects all future submissions. It does not change how past submissions were recorded.
</Warning>

To stop accepting submissions without deleting the form, toggle it to **Inactive**. Inactive forms show an error page if someone tries to access the hosted URL.

## Best practices

- **Ask only for what you need.** Fewer fields means higher completion rates. Start with name, email, and company — you can collect more information later.
- **Always map the email field.** Email is how the CRM identifies and deduplicates leads. A form without an email field will create leads that are hard to engage or deduplicate.
- **Set a default lead owner.** Unassigned leads often go unworked. Assign new form submissions to a specific rep or a round-robin rotation.
- **Test before you publish.** Submit the form yourself after embedding it to confirm the lead record is created with the correct data in the correct fields.
- **Connect new leads to sequences.** High-intent form submissions — like demo requests — benefit from immediate, automated follow-up via an [email sequence](/engage/email-sequences).
