Call logs are records of sales calls attached directly to CRM records. When you log a call, the details appear on the activity timeline of the associated lead or contact, giving your whole team a full picture of every conversation — who called, when, the outcome, and any notes taken.

Logging calls consistently keeps your pipeline accurate and makes handoffs between team members seamless.

## Log a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Navigate to the [lead](/records/leads) or [contact](/records/contacts) you called. Open their detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Log Call">
    In the activity section of the record, click **Log Call** (or **+ Activity → Call**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the call details">
    Complete the call log form:

    - **Date and time** — When the call took place.
    - **Duration** — How long the call lasted.
    - **Direction** — Whether you placed the call (outbound) or received it (inbound).
    - **Outcome** — What happened: Connected, Left voicemail, No answer, or a custom outcome.
    - **Notes** — A summary of what was discussed, next steps, or anything important for the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the log">
    Click **Save**. The call log appears immediately on the record's activity timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Log calls right after they happen while the details are fresh. Even a two-sentence summary of the conversation is far more useful than a blank outcome field.
</Tip>

## View call history on a record

Every logged call appears in the **Activity** tab of the associated record's detail page. The timeline shows:

- The call date and time
- Duration and direction (inbound/outbound)
- The outcome
- Notes added by the caller
- Which team member logged the call

You can see the full history of all calls for a lead or contact in one place, in chronological order. This is especially useful before a follow-up call — review the timeline to pick up exactly where the last conversation left off.

## Find and filter call logs

You can search and filter call logs from the **Engage → Call Logs** section in the sidebar:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Filter by date" icon="calendar">
    View calls from a specific day, week, or custom date range.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Filter by outcome" icon="check-circle">
    Narrow down to calls that connected, went to voicemail, or had no answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Filter by team member" icon="user">
    See calls logged by a specific rep or the whole team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search by record" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Find all calls associated with a specific lead or contact.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Use filters to pull reports on call activity — for example, how many calls a rep made this week, or how many leads were reached versus left a voicemail.

## Edit or delete a call log

To correct a mistake or add more detail after saving:

1. Open the record the call is logged on.
2. Find the call entry in the **Activity** timeline.
3. Click the **...** menu on the log and select **Edit** or **Delete**.

<Warning>
  Deleting a call log removes it permanently from the activity timeline. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Best practices

- **Log every call, even short ones.** A "called, no answer" entry takes seconds and prevents a teammate from calling the same prospect again the same day.
- **Use outcome fields consistently.** Standardized outcomes (Connected, Voicemail, No Answer) make team-level reporting meaningful.
- **Write useful notes.** Capture the key point of the conversation and the agreed-upon next step — not a transcript. "Interested, wants pricing by Friday. Follow up Thursday." is more actionable than a paragraph of detail.
- **Log calls before moving to the next task.** It's easy to forget details if you wait until end of day.
- **Review call logs before pipeline meetings.** Use the activity timeline to quickly brief yourself or your manager on recent activity for any deal.
