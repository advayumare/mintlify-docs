Workspace settings let you control how your CRM is configured for everyone in your organization. Access all of these settings from **Settings → Workspace**.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="General — workspace name, domain, and branding">

Access from: **Settings → Workspace → General**

The General Settings page lets you configure your workspace identity and branding, including the display name, logo, and domain.

### Workspace logo

- Click **Upload** to add your workspace logo.
  - Supported formats: **PNG, JPEG, GIF**
  - Recommended aspect ratio: **Square (1:1)**
  - Maximum size: **10 MB**
- Click **Remove** to delete the current logo and revert to the default workspace icon.

### Workspace name

Define the display name for your workspace. The name appears in:

- The application header
- Notifications
- Shared documents and links

<Warning>
  Choose a clear and recognizable name to make collaboration easier across teams.
</Warning>

### Domain

Set a unique workspace domain (for example, `yourcompany.crm.com`). The domain is used for:

- Workspace-specific URLs
- User login identification
- Shared resources and integrations

<Warning>
  Once a domain is set, changing it may affect integrations and shared links. Contact your administrator before updating.
</Warning>

### Best practices

- Use a high-resolution logo for the best appearance across the platform.
- Keep the workspace name consistent with your company or project identity.
- Set the domain carefully at the beginning of setup to avoid disruptions later.

</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Deal stages — customize your sales pipeline">

Access from: **Settings → Workspace → Deal Stages**

Deal stages define the steps your sales deals move through in the pipeline. They help your team understand where each deal stands and enable accurate forecasting and reporting.

### Manage deal stages

The deal stages list shows all stages currently available in your workspace. Each stage displays:

- **Name** — the stage name (e.g., New, Contacted, Qualified)
- **Type** — Standard or Custom
- **Status** — Active or Inactive
- **Actions** — Edit or Delete

Standard stages come pre-configured with the CRM. Custom stages are created by your team.

### Creating a deal stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + Create Deal Stage">
    Open the create stage form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name">
    Give the stage a clear, descriptive name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description">
    Explain the purpose of the stage so team members understand when to use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Deal Stage">
    The new custom stage appears in the list with **Type: Custom**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing a deal stage

Click the **Edit** icon next to a stage to update its name or description. Standard stages can be renamed but cannot be removed.

### Deleting a deal stage

Click the **Delete** icon to remove a custom stage.

<Warning>
  If any deals are currently assigned to a stage, you must reassign them before deleting it. Standard stages cannot be deleted.
</Warning>

### Stage types

- **Standard stages** — Built-in, foundational stages (e.g., New, Qualified, Negotiation) that maintain consistency across your organization.
- **Custom stages** — Created by administrators to support unique workflows or additional steps in your sales process.

### Best practices

- Keep stage names simple and easy to understand.
- Limit the number of stages to avoid pipeline clutter.
- Use descriptions so new team members understand each stage's purpose.
- Regularly review your stages to reflect improvements in your sales process.
- Align deal stages with Sales Pipeline Reporting for accurate forecasting.

</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Task statuses — customize task workflow labels">

Access from: **Settings → Workspace → Task Statuses**

Task statuses define the workflow stages your tasks can be in. They help your team track progress, organize work, and filter tasks based on their current state.

### Manage task statuses

The task statuses list shows all available statuses in your workspace. Each status includes:

- **Name** — the status name (e.g., Backlog, Todo, In Progress)
- **Type** — Standard or Custom
- **Status** — Active or Inactive
- **Actions** — Edit the status

Standard statuses are preconfigured. Custom statuses can be added by your team.

### Creating a task status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + Create Task Status">
    Open the create status form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    - **Name** — the status label
    - **Description** — explain when this status should be used
    - **Color** — choose a color to visually represent the status in the task list
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Task Status">
    The new status appears in the list as a **Custom** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing a task status

Click the **Edit** icon next to any status to update its name, description, or color. Standard statuses can be edited but **cannot be deleted**.

### Deleting task statuses

Only custom statuses can be deleted.

<Warning>
  If tasks are currently using a status you want to delete, you may need to reassign them first.
</Warning>

### Status types

**Standard statuses** — Built-in statuses that support common workflows:

- Backlog
- Todo
- In Progress
- Done
- Cancelled

**Custom statuses** — User-created statuses for specialized workflows.

### Best practices

- Keep statuses simple and easy to understand.
- Use consistent colors so team members can visually recognize task states.
- Avoid creating too many statuses — excessive complexity can slow down workflow clarity.
- Periodically review statuses to ensure they still match your team's workflows.

</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Departments — organize users by function">

Access from: **Settings → Workspace → Departments**

The Departments module lets you structure your workspace by grouping users into functional departments such as Engineering, HR, Sales, or Marketing. This improves reporting, permission management, and workflow organization.

### Department management

The departments list shows all departments in your workspace. Each row includes:

- **Department name**
- **Description**
- **Type** — Standard or Custom
- **Actions** — Edit and Delete

Departments you create are marked as **Custom**.

### Creating a department

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + Create Department">
    Open the create department form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the department name">
    The name is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description (optional)">
    Help team members understand the purpose of the department.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Department">
    The new department appears in the list as a **Custom** department.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing a department

Click the **Edit** icon to update the department name or description. Changes are applied immediately after saving.

### Deleting a department

Click the **Delete** icon to remove a department.

<Warning>
  If users are associated with the department, you may need to reassign them before deletion.
</Warning>

### Use cases

Departments can help organize:

- User groups based on function (Sales, HR, Engineering)
- Reporting and segmentation
- Permission assignments when combined with Roles & Permissions
- Workflow organization in large teams

### Best practices

- Keep department names consistent with your organization's structure.
- Use descriptions to clarify department responsibilities.
- Periodically review departments to keep your workspace clean and up-to-date.

</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Teams — create teams, assign leads, and view org chart">

Access from: **Settings → Workspace → Teams**

The Teams module lets you organize your workspace into structured groups, assign leads, manage members, and visualize your hierarchy using the built-in Org Chart.

### Overview

The Teams management page provides two views:

- **Table view** — a list of all teams including department, team lead, and member count
- **Org chart view** — a visual representation of team hierarchy and reporting structure

You can switch between views at any time from the top toolbar.

### Table view

The table lists all teams with the following information:

- **Team name**
- **Department**
- **Team lead**
- **Members count**
- **Actions** — Edit and Delete

### Creating a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create Team">
    Open the team creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the team name">
    Give the team a descriptive name (e.g., Engineering Team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description (optional)">
    Provide additional details about the team's purpose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a department">
    Choose the department this team belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the team lead">
    Select the person responsible for leading the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add team members (optional)">
    Add one or multiple members to the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Team">
    The team is created and appears in the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing a team

Click the **Edit** icon next to any team to update its name, description, department, team lead, or members. Click **Update Team** to save your changes.

### Deleting a team

Click the **trash** icon and confirm the deletion in the popup.

<Note>
  Deleting a team does not remove the users themselves — only the team grouping.
</Note>

### Org chart view

The Org Chart view provides a visual map of your workspace structure including:

- Team hierarchy
- Department grouping
- Reporting lines (Team Lead → Members)

You can:

- Zoom in and out
- Expand or collapse nodes
- Switch between horizontal and vertical orientation
- Hide or show the minimap

Each node (team, department, or member) is interactive. All changes to teams are reflected in the Org Chart in real time.

### Best practices

- Use departments to group teams logically.
- Assign a single team lead per team for clarity.
- Keep descriptions meaningful.
- Use the Org Chart regularly to verify structural accuracy.

</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — define your target customer">

Access from: **Settings → Workspace → Target ICP**

The Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) section helps you define the characteristics of the companies and decision-makers your business wants to target. Setting up your ICP helps the CRM generate better-quality leads, improve filtering, and personalize your outreach.

### What is an ICP?

An Ideal Customer Profile represents the type of customer most likely to benefit from your product or service. Setting clear ICP attributes keeps your sales and marketing teams aligned and focused on high-value prospects.

### ICP attributes

The ICP configuration includes five key attributes. Each field supports **multi-select**, so you can define multiple target segments. Your selections appear as labeled tags.

**Company size**

Specify the size of the companies you want to target:

- 1–10
- 11–50
- 51–200
- 200–500
- 500+

This helps refine lead generation by focusing on companies within your ideal operational scale.

**Target industry**

Select the industries relevant to your product or service. Examples:

- Information Technology
- Retail
- Real Estate
- Healthcare
- Manufacturing
- Dairy & Animal Farming

**Target locality**

Choose the geographical regions or countries you want to target. Examples:

- United States
- India
- United Kingdom
- Singapore
- American Samoa

You can select multiple locations to support global or regional targeting strategies.

**Target role / job title**

Define the key decision-makers or personas your outreach targets. Common roles include:

- CEO
- CTO
- VP Marketing
- CFO
- Head of Operations
- Founder

**Target revenue range**

Specify the annual revenue range of companies that fit your ICP:

- \< $100K
- $100K – $500K
- $1M – $5M
- $5M – $10M
- $10M+

### Saving your ICP

- Click **Save Changes** to save all modifications to your ICP configuration.
- Click **Reset** to revert all fields to their previously saved values.

### Best practices

- Keep your ICP updated as your business evolves.
- Review performance analytics to refine your ICP over time.
- Use multi-select fields to capture broader target segments without losing precision.
- Align ICP configuration with your marketing campaigns and sales messaging.
