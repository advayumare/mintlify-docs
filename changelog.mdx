<Update label="July 2025" description="v1.0.0">

# 1.0.0
--------------------------

Release date: July 2, 2026  
Codename: First Light

Thread CRM v1.0.0 is the first production release of the platform — a multi-tenant sales and marketing CRM built on a two-layer backend architecture: the Core Engine (platform services) and Business Modules (CRM features). Together they deliver a complete go-to-market stack: manage pipeline, run outbound campaigns, automate workflows, and engage across email, voice, WhatsApp, and social channels.

---

## Highlights

- Multi-tenant workspaces with isolated data, role-based access, and team collaboration
- Full CRM core — leads, contacts, companies, deals, tasks, notes, and activity timelines
- Multi-channel outbound — email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and AI voice call sequences
- Built-in AI — record-level copilot, template generation, and workflow AI agents
- Automation engine — visual workflows with triggers, branching, delays, and HTTP/code actions
- Developer-ready — OAuth API, workspace API keys, outbound webhooks, and connected apps

---

## Platform Foundation (Core Engine)

The Core Engine powers identity, tenancy, and shared infrastructure for every workspace.

### Identity & Access

- Email/password sign-up and sign-in with JWT sessions (access + refresh tokens)
- Social login via Google and Microsoft OAuth (PKCE)
- Optional email OTP verification and password reset flows
- Invite-link signup that can bypass OTP when the invite proves mailbox access
- Multi-workspace membership — users can belong to and switch between workspaces

### Workspace & Team

- Workspace activation with isolated database provisioning per tenant
- Custom workspace name, domain, subdomain, and logo branding
- Team invitations by email or public invite link, with role assignment
- Guided onboarding flow (profile setup, team invite, completion tracking)
- Default role configuration for new members

### Files & Storage

- Secure file uploads and downloads scoped to workspace
- Attachments on CRM records, profile pictures, and workspace logos
- Pluggable storage: local filesystem or S3-compatible object storage (AWS / MinIO)
- Optional auto-sync to Google Drive and OneDrive under organized folder paths

### Email Infrastructure

- Asynchronous email delivery with retry via background job queue
- Send via workspace SMTP settings or OAuth-connected Outlook accounts
- Transactional email for invites, OTP verification, and password reset

### Integrations & Admin

- OAuth 2.0 API for third-party apps with scoped access to contacts, companies, leads, and deals
- Platform Admin Panel for super-admins: workspace search, user management, and platform stats
- Impersonation for authorized support sessions with audit-friendly token claims
- Domain manager for workspace URL generation (subdomain + custom domain support)

### AI & Reliability

- Streaming AI chat endpoint with multi-provider support (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI)
- Per-workspace AI usage and cost tracking
- Background job processing (sync, PostgreSQL pg-boss, or Redis BullMQ)
- Global exception handling with optional Sentry reporting
- Health check endpoint (`/healthz`) for load balancers and deploy verification
- Redis-backed session storage for production

## CRM Core (Business Modules)

### Sales Pipeline

- Leads — full lifecycle management, search/filter, favorites, bulk update, soft delete
- Contacts (People) — import/export, duplicate validation, bulk create, advanced search
- Companies (Accounts) — owner assignment, related records, Google News RSS intel
- Deals — kanban pipeline with stage/position updates, lead-to-deal conversion, import/export
- Lists (Segments) — static lead/contact lists for campaigns and targeting
- Custom Fields — user-defined fields on any CRM entity with per-record values

### Engagement & Communication

- CRM Email Threads — send, reply, and log email conversations on lead/contact records with merge fields
- Unified Inbox (Workspace Email) — full mailbox with folders, search, drafts, forward/reply via Gmail, Outlook, or IMAP
- Email Templates — reusable templates with dynamic variables and AI content generation
- Email Signatures — per-user HTML signatures with default selection
- Email Health — deliverability monitoring, DNS/auth checks, blacklist status, and warmup controls
- Calls — call logging, export, browser-based dialing via Twilio, AI outbound calls via Vapi
- WhatsApp — connect WhatsApp Business via Meta OAuth, send/receive on lead records

### Marketing & Growth

- Marketing Campaigns — multi-step outbound sequences across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and AI voice
    - AI-generated sequence content, audience management, launch/pause/resume lifecycle
    - Open/link tracking, health scores, analytics, and engagement-triggered step conditions
    - Bulk sending via SendGrid, Brevo, or Smartlead
- Forms IQ — embeddable lead-capture forms with public submission endpoints, analytics, and A/B testing
- Visitors IQ — website visitor tracking via embeddable script with identity resolution and analytics
- Social Hub — publish to LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook; manage DMs, comments, and engagement
- Lead Scoring — configurable rules auto-scored from email events, form submissions, calls, meetings, and site visits

### Automation & Intelligence

- Workflows — visual automation builder with record triggers, schedules, webhooks, and manual launch
    - Actions: CRUD on records, send/draft email, HTTP requests, custom code, AI agent, delays, branching
    - Versioned drafts, publish lifecycle, run history, cancel/retry
- AI Copilot — contextual assistant on deals and leads: summaries, engagement insights, risks, next actions, follow-up drafts, calling scripts, and meeting briefs
- Data Enrichment — upload contact lists and enrich via Apollo and People Data Labs (PDL)

### Productivity

- Tasks — assignable tasks linked to any CRM record with configurable statuses
- Notes — rich notes attached to records with search and soft delete
- Activity Timeline — unified activity feed across all CRM objects
- Calendar — events linked to leads, contacts, and deals with Google/Outlook calendar sync
- Dashboard — customizable layouts with lead volume trends and deal summaries
- Notifications — in-app notification center with read/unread management
- Attachments & Drive — file attachments on records; Google Drive and OneDrive sync
- Reports — scheduled report definitions with on-demand send

### Administration

- Workspace Members & Teams — invite users, assign roles, organize sales teams
- Roles & Permissions — object-level CRUD permissions across all CRM modules
- Workspace Settings — configurable lead stages, deal stages, task statuses, currencies, and departments
- Audit Log — queryable trail of record changes
- Developer Settings — central hub for provider API keys (calling, email, AI, drive, enrichment), outbound webhooks, connected apps, and workspace API keys

## Architecture Notes

- Two-layer design: `CoreEngineModule` (21 platform modules) + `ModulesModule` (40+ feature modules)
- Multi-tenant isolation: each workspace gets its own data store
- Queue-driven async work: email, imports, campaigns, workflows, and webhooks run in background jobs
- Pluggable drivers: file storage (local/S3), message queue (sync/pg-boss/BullMQ), logging, and exception handling

---

## Known Limitations (v1.0.0)

- Opportunity module exists as a data model only — Deals is the active sales pipeline
- Two email surfaces: Messaging (CRM-logged threads on records) vs. Workspace Email (full personal inbox)
